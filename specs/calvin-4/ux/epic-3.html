When the example plan's emails go

Pacific time, as calvin shows every time. From 3.3's first acceptance criterion.

  1. Wed 11 Nov, 10:00 PMDecide by. Below 3: called off, and everyone gets E2. At 3 or more: it's on, and everyone gets E1, unless they already did when it confirmed.
  2. Fri 13 Nov, 10:00 AME3 "Tomorrow" to whoever is in then.
  3. Sat 14 Nov, 8:00 AME4 "Today" to whoever is in then.
  4. Sat 14 Nov, 10:00 AMIt starts. Call it off and withdraw stop working; the manage page loses Call it off (M4).

For the operator to decide before Epic 3 starts

  1. Manage page names. M1 follows 3.4: a full-name list and "2 of 3". Shaping's mockup 4 showed first-name seats and "You". Keep the list?
  2. Withdraw link in "Not this time" (E2). 3.1 requires it, but it lands on "nothing to withdraw from" (W1). Drop it from E2 only?
  3. Pill colours. The built app shows every pill in the orange "open" colour, including "It's on". These frames use ux-spec's green "on" and grey "off" pills. Should Epic 3 add them? (It's a small CSS change; 3.4 would carry it.)
  4. Buttons and link box. The built "ready to share" page uses unstyled Copy and Share buttons. The manage page needs ux-spec's secondary button, quiet link and link box (marked "New" in the CSS). Should 3.4 add them and restyle "ready to share" to match?
  5. Email bodies. Lines not fixed by the ACs are proposed (E1's "We'll remind you…", and E3 and E4 entirely). Approve them, or edit them here before 3.2 and 3.3 start.
  6. 3.6 heading. "Suggest a plan" (as built) or "Same again, new date"?
