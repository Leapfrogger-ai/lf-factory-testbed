calvin, Epic 3 screens: a visual design

Epic 3 is "Confirm, remind and call off". This sheet shows its six stories' screens at 360px, drawn before any of them starts. It also proposes a look for the whole app, so the built screens are shown too, restyled, with what would change in each one listed under it.

The frames and copy are the same as the first version of this PR. Copy is quoted from Epic 3's acceptance criteria where they fix it, otherwise from ux-spec.md, otherwise marked as proposed. What changed is the visual design: it starts from ux-spec.md's emotional goals (low stakes, easy yes, no blame), 360px first, and WCAG 2.2 AA, not from today's app.css.

Made with the frontend-design skill (Anthropic's plugin skill, followed from its SKILL.md). design-orchestrator wasn't used, because the 29 skills it routes to aren't installed here. Accessibility was checked by hand; the results are at the bottom.

Example plan throughout, as in the stories: Coffee at Blue Bottle, Oakland · Rockridge, Sat 14 Nov · 10:00 AM, minimum 3, decide by Wed 11 Nov, 10:00 PM. Ana Ruiz made it.

The direction

A plan passed around a group chat should feel like a note in a friend's handwriting, not a form from a booking site. Three choices carry that, and everything else stays quiet.

  • The seat row is the one bold element. Each person is a round token with their initial, and the empty seats are dashed, with the first one asking "you?". When the minimum is reached, the tokens turn green and a line joins them, like a table filling up. It's the only celebration in the app, as ux-spec asks, and the only thing that moves (once, and not with reduced motion on).
  • A serif for the plan, a rounded sans for everything else. The venue is set large in the phone's own book serif (Iowan Old Style or Charter on iPhone and Mac, Georgia elsewhere), so the plan reads like something written down. The rest stays in ux-spec's system rounded sans. No web font is downloaded, so the public page keeps its ≤ 2s budget.
  • Berry instead of terracotta. The warm off-white and terracotta in ux-spec §4.1 is the most common look of generated pages right now. A soft lilac-grey paper with one berry accent keeps it warm and casual but specific to calvin, and berry stays well clear of the error red, so "Not this time" can never read as a failure.

Dark theme tokens follow prefers-color-scheme, as ux-spec asks; use the buttons above to see every frame in dark. The proposed stylesheet is the .app block at the top of this file's CSS, about 9 KB unminified, under ux-spec's 10 KB target.

What changes in the built screens
ColoursNew palette in both themes (above). The token names change, so app.css is rewritten, not patched. No copy changes.
TypeVenue and page titles move to the system serif. The venue grows from 26px to 36px. Body text is unchanged.
State pillEach state gets its own style: berry "Needs N more", green "It's on", grey "Not this time", green outline "Happening now". Today every pill is orange.
Seat row14px dots become 40px tokens with the person's initial (the initial is hidden from screen readers; the list label is unchanged).
Where and when"Oakland · Sat 14 Nov · 10:00 AM" splits into two lines: the time in bold, the city under it. Same words.
Wordmark"calvin" appears at the top of every page, as ux-spec §5 says. Today only the create page has it.
NoticesNotices move to the top of the content (ux-spec §8.2). Today the public page puts them under the seats.
Ready to shareCopy and Share stop being unstyled browser buttons: a link box with Copy inside it, Share as a secondary button, and Manage your plan as a quiet link.
Kept as isAll copy, the order of things on each page, the 440px single column, no images or web fonts, flat surfaces with no shadows, 48px controls, the focus ring.

Accessibility, checked by hand

Against ux-spec §9.2 (WCAG 2.2 AA), on this sheet's frames.

ContrastAll 24 text and boundary pairs pass in both themes (48 checks, computed from the hex values). The lowest text pair is "It's on" green on its tint, 5.5:1 in light; the lowest boundary is input borders on the page, 3.35:1 against a 3:1 floor.
Colour aloneEvery state is also named in words ("Needs 1 more", "It's on", "Not this time"). Open and on pills also differ by a dot; the off pill has none.
Tap targetsButtons and inputs are at least 48px tall; Copy inside the link box, Edit, Change, Keep it, Call it off and Suggest a plan are at least 44px.
Keyboard and focusEvery control is a native link, button, input or <details>, in reading order, with a 2px berry ring at 2px offset (7.0:1 on paper).
Screen readersSeat initials and the decorative empty row are aria-hidden; the lists keep ux-spec's labels ("2 of 3 people in", "Who's in"). Notices keep role="status". One <h1> per screen.
MotionOne animation: the seat line drawing in on "It's on". It runs only under prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference.
360pxNo frame scrolls sideways at 360px; long names ellipsise in seats and wrap in the manage list.

For the operator to decide before Epic 3 starts

  1. Manage page names. M1 follows 3.4: full names and "2 of 3". Shaping's mockup 4 showed first-name seats. The design answers this: full names in a list, with the same tokens as the public seats, so it follows 3.4 and still looks like the same app. Approve?
  2. Withdraw link in "Not this time" (E2). 3.1 requires it, but it lands on "nothing to withdraw from" (W1). Drop it from E2 only? Not a design question; still open.
  3. Colours and pills. The built app shows every pill in orange, including "It's on". The design adds green "on" and grey "off" pills, and goes further: a new palette (berry on lilac-grey) and a serif for the venue, replacing ux-spec §4.1 and §4.2. Approve the new look, or keep ux-spec's terracotta and take only the pill colours?
  4. Buttons and link box. The manage page needs a secondary button, quiet links and a link box that app.css doesn't have. The design draws them, and restyles every built screen to match (B1–B5, P1, P2, W1). Should 3.4 carry the whole restyle, or should it be a separate story ahead of 3.4?
  5. Email bodies. Lines not fixed by the ACs are proposed (E1's "We'll remind you…", and E3 and E4 entirely). Approve them, or edit them here before 3.2 and 3.3 start. Not a design question; still open.
  6. 3.6 heading. "Suggest a plan" (as built) or "Same again, new date"? Still yours. From the design side, "Same again, new date" says what the page is for, since it isn't suggesting anything.
